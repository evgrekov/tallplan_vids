- Graphic numbers/counters as visual elements are welcome when they fit the idea (e.g. glowing 1-2-3 orbiting the character); never words or sentences on screen.
- Exaggerate the problem: whatever the VO talks about must be shown unmistakably and a bit stronger than in reality (e.g. clearly visible thick body hair, an obviously sharp jawline), so it reads at a glance on a phone.

- Safety filter: the character is a young ADULT (19–20 years old, never a teen/minor age), especially in body close-ups. - Style refs for the character: timecodes of 2 reference frames where the main character is biggest and clearest (no text-heavy or before/after frames).
