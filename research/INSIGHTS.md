What repeats in all of them:
- 3D character with a skull head (5 different looks), AI male voiceover 185–210 wpm, quiet music bed under non-stop speech.
- One word on screen at a time; "Day N" titles in the 30-day videos.
- Visual changes every ~1.5 s; picture literally shows the spoken word (milk → glass body fills, calcium → tiny workers).
- Generic hashtags (#fyp #educational #skeleton), the hook question often duplicated in the description.
- The channel keeps changing the character — no stable mascot, and no second hit.

What the hit had and the rest do not:
- **Promise about the viewer's own body** ("if you want to increase your height") + result shown in the first second (grows next to a ruler). Everything else is "what happens if…" or third-person comparison.
- **Saveable numbered list** (3 exercises with how-to) → 4.4% saves vs 0.2–0.7% everywhere else.
- **Honest twist** (over 21 it is hard; posture gives 1–2 in) → ~160 of 389 comments post height + age.
- Short: 50 s vs 64–92 s for the rest.
- The same glass character on the same day without the promise and list ("sleep on the floor", also about standing taller) got 6x, not 2000x → the script made the hit, not the character.

Second-best (backrooms, 25x): entertainment, meme stereotypes + trending setting + twist ending. Not our use case, but shows that versus/race with recognizable types beats plain facts on this channel.

Height topic on this channel: "how to look taller" = 2037x; "sleep on the floor → stand taller" = 6x; "tall vs short in the gym" = 3x.
The 10–14 y.o. audience reacts to a personal promise + steps, not to comparisons.

Ideas for TallPlan:
- 🟢 Hook = promise about the viewer's height + visible result in the first second.
- 🟢 Numbered list of 3 actions with how-to (saves), ≤50 s, finish with an honest truth + question that invites "height + age" comments.
- 🟢 Keep: skull/x-ray or glass character, word-by-word captions, ~200 wpm voice, literal visuals, inside-body metaphors.
- 🟡 Day 1→30 format: steady 5–8x, never a breakout.
- 🟡 Versus with meme stereotypes (mogger / gamer) can work as a reach play.
- 🔴 Avoid third-person "tall guy vs short guy" framing for height.

## Height niche: 9 channels (2026-11-21)

Channels: @heightible @generationalglowuptiktok @gotall.abdul @gotallkae @dobbingotall @gotall.scribi @tallrizz @geezhw_ (@brady.gotall is banned).
Rows: RefVids 10–29, Stats snapshot 2026-11-21, channel numbers in Refs / ChannelStats.

| Format | Best | ER | Saves | Comments | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Selfie + height table overlay, 5–15 s, music only | 2M / 1.8M / 1.4M | 4.5–8.1% | 0.3–0.5% | 0.4–1.5% | heightible, gotall.abdul, glowup, gotallkae |
| Face cam "3 signs…" listicle, 24–49 s | 112K / 80K / 72K | 6–10.7% | 1.5–2.3% | ~0.1% | dobbingotall, gotall.scribi |
| Photo carousel: ideal height for age, x/10 tiers, anime boy | 344K / 256K / 226K | 1.5–4.2% | 0.1–0.4% | 0.2–0.4% | tallrizz |
| Sarcastic reverse-tips carousel + follow meme | 328K / 185K / 1.6K | 2.3–3.7% | 0.5–0.8% | 0.1–0.2% | geezhw_ |

What drives each:
- **Selfie + table** = reach. The viewer finds their own row (age → height) in 1 s; comments are height + age (789 of 1056 on the glowup 622K). Same sound reused across hits (heightible 1.8M and 1.4M). gotall.abdul adds the app screen at the end and still gets 1.4M.
- **"3 signs" talking head** = saves. "Save this and send to a short friend" in the first 5 s → 1.5–2.3% saves, the best in the niche after skeleton 1.1M (4.4%). The app is one of the 3 items, not a separate ad.
- **tallrizz carousels** = rating that places the viewer (10/10 crown → 2/10 crying). The app slide sits in the middle, so people swipe through it. Long honest captions ("late bloomers sit below the line") + "comment your height".
- **geezhw_** = humor works once: the joke hit 115x on day 1, the 2nd repeat 65x, the 3rd 0.6x.

Ideas for TallPlan:
- 🟢 Selfie/character + age→height table overlay, ≤12 s, loud trending sound, no voice — cheapest reach format.
- 🟢 "3 signs / 3 things no one tells you" with "save this and send to a short friend" in the hook; app as item #2 or #3.
- 🟢 Carousel with x/10 tiers for one age ("ideal height at 14"), app slide in the middle, caption asks for height + age.
- 🟡 Reverse/sarcastic tips: one-shot reach, do not repeat the same joke.
- 🔴 Separate ad at the end of a long video — every hit hides the app inside the content.

What works:
- **One template, many final heights.** Each video = one target (170, 173, 183, 198, 201 cm); a viewer finds "his" number. Script is identical, only numbers change → cheap to mass-produce.
- **Year-by-year with % grown** (88% at 13, 95% at 15) + "turbo mode" + "genes set your range" — honest, concrete, comment bait "age + height".
- **Add-on angle per video**: dad, country averages, parent formula, one exercise per age — keeps the template fresh.
- **Carousels that make the viewer act** (measure hand, check parents) → 1.2–1.4% saves; the app is the last slide.
- AI voice 100–140 wpm over slowed phonk; video length 40–83 s still works because numbers keep changing every 2–3 s.

Ideas for TallPlan (own style):
- 🟢 Series "Final height X: year by year path" with our character instead of the pill, one video per height 160–201 cm; TallPlan prediction screen as the last card.
- 🟢 Variants: vs dad, vs country averages, parent formula, exercise per age.
- 🟢 Poster carousels "measure your hand / parents combos" with TallPlan slide at the end.
- 🟡 Keep ≤60 s; the 40 s video got the highest ER of the November batch.
